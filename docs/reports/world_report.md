## How to read the map

- Continents sit where they live: **eu/na** north-west, **as/oc** east, **sa/af** south, **an** at the bottom.
- Larger filled circles are **super controllers (masters)**; smaller ones are **super peers**.
- Dashed golden lines are the observer's own serverless links to the swarm — every line is a real authenticated socket.
- Gaze sits at the centre as a silent, equal node; it never routes, never owns, never commands alone.
