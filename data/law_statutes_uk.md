# UK Magna Carta 1297 cl. 29
Tag: due process, liberty

No Freeman shall be taken or imprisoned, or be disseised of his Freehold, or Liberties, or free Customs, or be outlawed or exiled, or any other ways destroyed; nor will We not pass upon him, nor condemn him, but by lawful Judgment of his Peers, or by the Law of the Land. We will sell to no man, we will not deny or defer to any man either Justice or Right.

# UK Bill of Rights 1689 art. 1
Tag: rule of law, parliament

That the pretended Power of Suspending of Laws or the Execution of Laws by Regal Authority without Consent of Parliament is illegal.

# UK Bill of Rights 1689 art. 4
Tag: taxation, parliament

That levying Money for or to the Use of the Crowne, by Pretence of Prerogative, without Grant of Parlyament, for a longer time, or in other manner, than the same is or shall be granted, is Illegal.

# UK Bill of Rights 1689 art. 9
Tag: speech, parliament

That the Freedome of Speech and Debates or Proceedings in Parlyament ought not to be impeached or questioned in any Court or Place out of Parlyament.

# UK Act of Settlement 1701 s. 2
Tag: succession, crown

That whosoever shall hereafter come to the Possession of this Crown shall joyn in Communion with the Church of England as by Law established; and that every Person who shall hereafter come to the Possession of this Crown shall profess the Protestant Reformed Religion as established by Law, and if any Person shall afterwards profess the Popish Religion or marry a Papist, they shall for ever be uncapable to inherit, possess or enjoy the Crown.

# UK Human Rights Act 1998 s. 3
Tag: interpretation, convention rights

So far as it is possible to do so, primary legislation and subordinate legislation must be read and given effect in a way which is compatible with the Convention rights.

# UK Human Rights Act 1998 Sch. 1 art. 9
Tag: religion, belief

Everyone has the right to freedom of thought, conscience and religion; this right includes freedom to change his religion or belief and freedom, either alone or in community with others and in public or private, to manifest his religion or belief, in worship, teaching, practice and observance.

# UK Human Rights Act 1998 Sch. 1 art. 10
Tag: expression, freedom

Everyone has the right to freedom of expression. This right shall include freedom to hold opinions and to receive and impart information and ideas without interference by public authority and regardless of frontiers.
